How it works

  1. Start a trip

    Open Peerelle and begin a guided capture for pickup or return—no sign-in required.

  2. Follow the lens

    Step through prompts for exterior and key areas so photos stay consistent from trip to trip.

  3. Review on device

    Check your shots before you finish. Everything is saved locally on your phone.

  4. Keep your record

    Your photos remain in the app and on your device. Peerelle does not upload them to our servers.

Privacy by design

Photos stay on your device. Peerelle is built for local-first capture—no Peerelle account, no advertising, and no analytics SDKs that track you across apps. Camera access is used only to take inspection photos you choose to capture. If you allow location for geotags on photos, that data stays with the photos on your device.
