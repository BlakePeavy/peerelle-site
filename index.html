Guided rental-vehicle photos, kept on your phone

Peerelle walks hosts and renters through before-and-after inspection photos so both sides get a consistent record at pickup and return.

  • No account
  • No ads or tracking

How it works

  1. Start a trip

    Open Peerelle and begin a guided capture for pickup or return. No sign-in required.

  2. Follow the lens

    Step through prompts for the exterior and key areas so shots stay consistent from trip to trip.

  3. Review on device

    Check your shots before you finish. Everything is saved on your phone.

  4. Keep your record

    Your photos stay in the app on your device. Peerelle does not upload them.

Photos stay on your phone

No Peerelle account, no ads, and no analytics SDKs that track you across apps. Camera access is only used when you take inspection photos. If you allow location for geotags, that data stays with the photos on your device.
