Support

Help for Peerelle—guided rental-vehicle photo capture that keeps photos on your device.

FAQ

How do I start a trip?

Open Peerelle and choose to begin a guided capture for pickup or return. Follow the on-screen prompts to photograph each area. No account or sign-in is required.

Where do my photos go?

Photos stay on your device. Peerelle does not upload inspection photos to Peerelle servers. If you share or export photos using other apps, that sharing is under your control and those apps’ policies.

How do I delete my photos or Peerelle data?

Remove photos from within the app if that option is available, clear the app’s storage in your device settings, or uninstall Peerelle. Because data is local, uninstalling or clearing app data typically deletes Peerelle’s on-device content. Peerelle cannot restore data that only existed on your phone.

Why does Peerelle need camera permission?

Camera access is required to take guided inspection photos. Peerelle uses the camera only when you capture photos in the app. You can revoke permission in system settings; without it, you will not be able to take new photos in Peerelle.

Does Peerelle use my location?

Location is optional. If you allow it for geotags on photos, that information stays with the photos on your device. Peerelle does not send location to Peerelle. You can deny location and still use guided capture.

Do I need an account?

No. Peerelle does not require a Peerelle account and does not use the app to create one.

Is Peerelle affiliated with Turo or my insurer?

No. Peerelle is an independent tool. It is not affiliated with Turo, other rental platforms, or insurance companies, and it does not provide insurance or legal advice. See our Terms of Use.
